================================================================================
AMBIENT STUDY COMMONS: API & AI ROLES OVERVIEW
================================================================================

1. WHAT APIS WOULD A FULLY OPERATIONAL BACKEND CALL?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If fully operational with a live backend, the system would connect to:

* Canvas LMS API:
  - Pulls active course enrollments, syllabi, assignment due dates, and module topics
    so the system knows what courses the student is studying and what deadlines are coming up.

* Foundation Model APIs (Google Gemini / OpenAI):
  - Handles all language reasoning: summarizing daily study sessions, generating vector
    embeddings for skill/dream matching, and running the live AI Host in study rooms.

* Google Identity / OAuth2 API:
  - Provides safe, 1-click student sign-in using their verified school Google account.

* Calendar & Notification APIs (Google Calendar, Web Push, SendGrid):
  - Sends notifications when a peer confirms a match, and automatically syncs 
    confirmed study meetings to students' Google Calendars.


2. WHERE & HOW IS AI USED IN THE PROGRAM?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AI is used across 6 practical roles to turn solitary study into peer collaboration:

1. Session Summarizer (Browser Extension Widget):
   - When the user asks their AI (ChatGPT or Gemini) for help, the extension extracts 
     key takeaways, bugs debugged, and next steps into a concise session note.

2. Daily Master Journal Synthesizer (Nightly Process):
   - At the end of the day, AI combines fragmented daytime chat notes with Canvas 
     homework progress into one clean Daily Master Journal, celebrating daily effort.

3. Skill & Growth Profiler (skillCheck):
   - Analyzes problem-solving patterns to identify real strengths, growth areas, 
     and team roles (e.g. Systems Builder vs. Creative Designer) rather than relying on dry resumes.

4. Passion & Dream Tracker (dreamCheck):
   - Detects recurring personal project ideas mentioned in chats (e.g., "setting up a Minecraft 
     server", "building a 3D simulation") and tracks interest count over time.

5. Smart Peer Matchmaker:
   - Pairs students who have complementary skills, shared Canvas classes, overlapping 
     free-time schedules, and matching dream topics. It also writes a plain-English 
     "Synergy Highlight" explaining why the two should team up.

6. AI Host Owl (Greeting Room Facilitator):
   - Presides over private student study rooms: welcomes both students by name, breaks 
     the ice with shared goals, provides course milestone agendas, and supports their discussion.

7. Weekly Review & Confidence Builder:
   - Packages the week's hard work into a reflective retrospective, helping students 
     feel recognized, build confidence, and plan the upcoming week.
