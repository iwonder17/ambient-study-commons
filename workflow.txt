                 From Solo AI Study Sessions to Dream Collaboration

Target Audience: Hackathon Judges & Reviewers | Reading Time: ~60 seconds

DEMO STATUS: This prototype illustrates the intended system using sample student
data, simulated integrations, and sample AI outputs. The workflow below describes
the proposed backend, including live API calls and scheduled processing.


1. THE PROBLEM & VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Solo Study Trap: Students spend hours learning with AI (ChatGPT, Gemini), but 
  their knowledge and project ideas stay buried in messy, forgotten chat threads.
• The Solution: A privacy-first ambient Chrome extension + dashboard that turns 
  daily AI chats into reflective journals, maps your skills, and connects you 
  with complementary peers to build your dream projects.

AI & API Backbone:
• Canvas APIs supply consented course, assignment, and academic context.
• Model APIs analyze study notes, journals, and academic context to build evolving
  profiles of demonstrated skills, interests, and project goals. These profiles
  drive peer matching, match explanations, and AI-hosted introductions.
• Planned activity-search APIs find relevant opportunities from those interests;
  a provider has not yet been specified.

3. 6-STEP USER JOURNEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ambient Capture: An unobtrusive owl mascot on ChatGPT/Gemini injects grounded 
   reflection prompts and captures study notes. Canvas LMS context syncs with consent.
2. Nightly Synthesis: At 8:00 PM, AI consolidates messy daytime chats into one 
   cohesive master journal, updates your skill roles, and tracks project dreams.
3. Weekly Reflection: Clicking "Weekly Review" copies your week's journals with 
   a cozy reflection prompt straight into your clipboard to explore in your favorite AI.
4. Dream Pool Trigger: When a project idea is mentioned repeatedly (e.g. 5x), the 
   mascot prompts: "You've been thinking about this a lot! Want to find teammates?"
5. Double-Opt-In Match: Matches peers on goal alignment, complementary
   strengths, and free-time overlap. Meetings are booked ONLY if both peers say "Yes".
6. AI Greeting Room: 5 hours before, a reminder check-in confirms attendance. At meeting 
   time, AI Host Owl introduces both students, breaks the ice, and hands off contacts.

Matching Score Note: The proposed >=80% goal-alignment threshold is a design
target, not a validated compatibility probability. The intended engine evaluates
shared project goals and interests, complementary skills, and free-time overlap.
The scoring formula, weights, and threshold still need implementation and validation;
the demo's sample scores illustrate the experience.


4. PRIVACY, SAFETY & ANTI-FLAKE GUARDRAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Granular Student Consent: Independent toggles for AI chats, Canvas modules, and grades.
• Zero Creepy Browsing: No public profile search. Matches are strictly backend-curated.
• Local-First Privacy: 100% data export and permanent wipe controls on the dashboard.
• Private Credit Score: Attendance is tracked privately. Flaking or ghosting lowers 
  invitation priority, naturally protecting reliable students without public shaming.
